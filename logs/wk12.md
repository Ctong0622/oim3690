## API mashup

A tool for art lovers that takes weather data from Open-Meteo and artwork information from the Art Institute of Chicago API, and asks AI to recommend artworks and plan an indoor art exhibition visit based on the weather.

## Final project ideas

1. AI Car Buying Advisor: A website that uses AI to recommend used cars based on a user's budget, driving needs, and preferences, and explains the pros and cons of each option.
2. AI Study Plan Generator: A website that uses AI to create personalized study schedules based on students' courses, deadlines, and available time.
3. AI Travel Itinerary Planner:A website that uses AI to create personalized travel plans based on a user's destination, budget, interests, and trip length.
