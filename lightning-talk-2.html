Apple Siri AI

Apple is rebuilding Siri to go beyond answering questions — toward actually taking action across your apps.

Announced for developers

App Intents & Third‑Party Actions

Apple has announced that developers will be able to let Siri trigger actions inside their apps — like playing a song, editing a photo, or sending a message — using the App Intents framework.

Available today

Everyday Siri Features

Right now, everyone already has Siri for setting timers, sending texts, checking the weather, and controlling smart home devices — the basics that ship on every iPhone.

The deeper "agentic" Siri capabilities are still rolling out — not every feature below is live for all users yet.

What Could Change

From Answers to Actions

Instead of just telling you information, Siri could soon act on it — here are three potential use cases.

📦

Checking a Package

Ask Siri where your delivery is, and it could pull live status from the shipping app itself — no need to open it.

🍽️

Restaurant Reservations

Siri might be able to book you a table through a reservation app, matching your time and party size automatically.

🛍️

Helping with Purchases

From reordering a product to completing checkout, Siri could assist with parts of a purchase inside supported apps.

These are potential use cases based on Apple's announced direction for Siri — not confirmed, guaranteed, or fully live features.
